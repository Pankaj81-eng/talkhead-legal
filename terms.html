Scope of this service

TalkHead Newsletter is a personal automation, built and operated solely by its author, Pankaj Kumar, that generates and publishes one short AI-news video per day to accounts the author owns (YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok). It is not a public product, is not offered for sign-up, and is not intended for use by anyone other than its author. There is no user registration, account creation, or public-facing service surface for anyone to accept these terms as an end user of — this page exists to satisfy platform API review requirements for the OAuth apps the pipeline connects to.

What the automation does

Each run reads one publicly available AI-industry news story, writes a short narration script from it, synthesizes narration in the author's own voice, assembles a short video, and — only after the author personally approves it via a private Telegram message — posts that video to the author's own social accounts. No step in this pipeline collects data from, or posts on behalf of, any account other than the author's own.

Content & accuracy

Published videos summarize real, publicly reported news in plain language. While the pipeline includes automated fact-checking steps, it is not a substitute for the original reporting it summarizes, and viewers should treat it as a summary, not a primary source.

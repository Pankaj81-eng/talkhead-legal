Daily AI-news video

The day's biggest AI story, explained in a minute.

TalkHead Newsletter turns each day's most important AI-industry story into a short, plain-language video — no jargon, no assumed background, just what happened and why it matters. One new video every day, published across YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok.

How it works

Every day, the same simple pipeline runs automatically:

  1. Reads the day's top AI-industry news story from a fixed editorial source.
  2. Writes a short, plain-language narration script — the kind you could explain to a friend who's never followed AI news.
  3. Synthesizes narration and assembles a short vertical video with relevant visuals and captions.
  4. Sends the finished video for a one-tap human review before anything goes live.
  5. Once approved, publishes the video to the channels below.

Who runs it

TalkHead is an automated content pipeline for a real, public channel, built and operated by its author, Pankaj Kumar. There's no public sign-up or multi-user accounts — the pipeline itself has a single operator — but the videos it publishes are public content on YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok for anyone to watch.
